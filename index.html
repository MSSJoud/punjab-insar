Ground deformation in Punjab from Sentinel-1 InSAR

Interactive maps of vertical and east ground motion, 2016–2024, from three Sentinel-1 tracks (one ascending, two descending). Click anywhere on a map to see the monthly time series at that point.

Deformation explorer Vertical, east and line-of-sight velocity maps with clickable monthly time series. Oct 2016 – Jan 2024. About 25 MB, so it takes a few seconds to load. Deformation explorer: full period Same maps over the longest available window. Jan 2016 – May 2024. About 25 MB. Data quality explorer Temporal coherence, unwrapping connectivity and line-of-sight velocity for each track, with adjustable quality masks. About 42 MB; best on a desktop.
Processed from Copernicus Sentinel-1 data. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.
